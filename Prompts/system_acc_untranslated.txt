Detect only non translated errors: error occurring when a text segment that was intended for translation is left untranslated in the target content, except person and entities names should not be translated. Exemplos: A sentence in an English document translated into Portuguese is left in English. “What are your key performance indicators ?” translated into “Quais são seus key performance indicators ?” (everything should be translated)
